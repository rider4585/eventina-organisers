Leadership & Creative Direction

The Visionaries Behind Eventina

Meet the minds uniting architectural spatial design, five-star luxury hospitality, and arena-grade technical production into every celebration.

The Leadership Philosophy

Where Artistic Audacity Meets Flawless Execution

At Eventina, our founding partners don’t delegate creative vision to distant subcontractors. Each commission is directly stewarded by our directors, ensuring that every nuance of aesthetic curation, acoustic staging, and guest hospitality reflects your distinct standards.

Co-Founder & Creative Director

Arjun Mehta

Trained as an architectural designer, Arjun spent over a decade reimagining historic palace grounds, private islands, and luxury hotel ballrooms into breathtaking narrative landscapes. He leads spatial planning, custom floral architecture, and mood curation for Eventina’s signature events.

“Every venue holds an innate character. Our duty is to elevate it with sculptural florals, light, and timeless proportions.”
Spatial Scenography Floral Architecture Palace Venues
Co-Founder & Managing Director

Ananya Rao

With a background in luxury hotel management and VIP diplomatic protocol, Ananya orchestrates multi-city travel logistics, luxury charter fleets, and bespoke guest concierges with calm mastery. She ensures that hosts and guests experience complete tranquility.

“True luxury is effortless on the surface because hundreds of moving parts have been harmonized beforehand with clockwork care.”
Turnkey Hospitality Destination Logistics VIP Concierge
Partner & Head of Production

Vikramaditya Sengupta

A veteran of high-voltage concert production, international film festivals, and broadcast galas, Vikramaditya commands the technical spine of Eventina. He oversees acoustic engineering, motorized kinetic staging, and celebrity artist hospitality riders.

“Magic in live production is born from precision. When acoustic clarity and lighting synchrony hit in unison, the energy in the room is electrifying.”
Concert AV Systems Kinetic Stagecraft Artist Liaison
Our Guiding Creed

The Eventina Leadership Commitments

Principles that govern every relationship, production decision, and celebration we direct.

Direct Partner Stewardship

We take on a strictly limited number of commissions per calendar season. Arjun, Ananya, or Vikramaditya is physically present at every major walkthrough, rehearsal, and live production.

Discreet VIP Protection

From prominent business families to celebrity icons, privacy is sacrosanct. We maintain strict non-disclosure agreements, encrypted guest manifests, and perimeter access controls.

Zero Commission Bias

We recommend venues, florists, and artists based solely on aesthetic and operational excellence. Our vendor curation is transparent, unbiased, and aligned completely with your interests.

Schedule a private creative consultation to explore concepts, feasibility, and custom scenography for your upcoming celebration.
