However, our turnkey production architecture allows us to produce extraordinary multi-day celebrations on an expedited 3-to-4-month timeframe when required, utilizing our established vendor network and pre-audited technical infrastructure.

We operate on a transparent, flat professional management fee determined by event scale, guest count, and the complexity of multi-day production.

Unlike traditional event brokers who inflate costs with invisible supplier commissions, we pass all negotiated venue, technical, and floral rates directly to you with zero back-end markups. You receive original vendor invoices and transparent ledger accounting.

While our signature strength is complete turnkey execution (creative scenography, technical staging, and guest hospitality under one accountable director), we welcome collaboration with your preferred family couturiers, fine-art photographers, or trusted culinary advisors.

We integrate external partners into our master technical cue sheets, provide them with dedicated backstage support, and coordinate power and logistical requirements smoothly.

We conduct comprehensive on-ground technical recce visits alongside the hosts. We do not just look at aesthetics—we audit generator backup redundancy, kitchen banquet capacities, acoustic reverberation in marble courtyards, and load-in elevator dimensions.

Leveraging our ongoing enterprise volume with luxury hotel chains (Taj, Oberoi, Leela, ITC, Marriott), we negotiate favorable buyout rates, complimentary room upgrades, and extended event curation curfews directly with property general managers.

Every outdoor event designed by Eventina incorporates a fully detailed “Plan B” with matching aesthetic caliber. We do not treat indoor backups as an afterthought.

We secure parallel indoor ballrooms or maintain rapid-deployment structural marquees with climate-controlled heating/cooling on standby. Our technical director tracks real-time satellite radar, making informed, seamless transition calls with the family four hours prior to guest entry if necessary.

Our commissions typically range from INR 40 Lakhs for bespoke single-day corporate galas and private soirées, to INR 1.5 Crores to over INR 15 Crores for multi-day residential destination weddings.

During your confidential initial consultation, we formulate an itemized financial framework that balances your creative vision with realistic vendor allocations, ensuring zero budgetary surprises downstream.

Our artist management wing negotiates directly with celebrity talent management, cutting out secondary sub-agents. We handle legal contracts, performance riders, five-star hospitality suites, and backstage security protocols.

More importantly, our in-house audio engineers review and fulfill complex digital console technical riders, providing touring artists with concert-grade sound checks completed hours before guests arrive.

We set up private airport arrival desks staffed by multilingual concierges. Guests are escorted directly into luxury chauffeur fleets, with luggage tagged and transferred to their designated suites without guest intervention.

Our custom guest management desk tracks dietary preferences, elderly mobility requirements, and customized welcome gifting suites.

We assume 100% legal and statutory responsibility for all event permissions. This includes Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) clearances for heritage forts, local traffic commissioner NOCs, fire department safety audits, and municipal decibel adherence approvals.

We also secure excise liquor permits, PPL/IPRS music licensing, and DGCA drone aviation permissions, ensuring your celebration proceeds without statutory interruption.

We intentionally cap our schedule to a maximum of two major celebrations per calendar month.
