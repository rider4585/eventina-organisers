computers.

The first page visitors see, with an introduction to your company
2. About Us
Your story and what makes you special

2  Website Analytics Setup
 ₹3,000
In simple words: analytics tells you how many people visit your website, which pages they look at,
where they come from (search engines, social media, etc.) and which city they are in.
For ₹3,000 we will:
  Add the tracking to your website so that your visitors are counted
 Set up the analytics so that your visitor numbers are recorded and available as reports
Showing these visitor numbers inside your admin dashboard is part of the admin dashboard quotation and will be
priced there, when the dashboard is built.

3  Admin Dashboard
 Separate quotation
In simple words: the admin dashboard is a private, password-protected area where your team can
manage parts of the website and, over time, other parts of your business.
Please note
The admin dashboard is charged separately and its details and pricing will be shared in a separate
Admin Dashboard quotation. The price in this quotation for the Blogs section (and posts) covers
building that module on the website. It does not include building the dashboard that is used to write
and manage the blogs. That dashboard work will be described in the separate quotation.
4  Website Content
What we do: while building your website, we will use sample (mock) text and images as
placeholders, so that you can see how the final website will look and work.
What you provide: the final content must be given by you. This includes final text, the images for
services and artists, portfolio and event photos (including photos for the map pins), founder details,
testimonials with client images, FAQs, your logo and social media links. We will also need access to
your online business listing, so that your customer reviews can be shown on the website. We will
replace the sample content with your final content before the website is published.
5  Updating Website Content
 Before the admin dashboard is ready: we (the developers) will be responsible for updating the
content on the website for you.
 After the admin dashboard is ready: only the things supported by the dashboard modules can
be updated by your team from the dashboard.
 Things the dashboard cannot update will still be updated by us. Depending on the change, it will
be treated either as maintenance or as new development. We will tell you which one it is, and
discuss any cost with you, before we start.
6  Search Engine (SEO) Setup
 Included
In simple words: SEO means setting up your website so that search engines can understand it and
show it when people search for event organisers. Basic SEO is included:
  A clear website structure that search engines can easily read
 A clear title and short description for each page, so people know what they will find in search results
 Clearly organised page headings and descriptions for your images, so search engines understand
your content
 A list of all your pages given to search engines, so that none of them are missed
 Basic speed improvements so that your pages load quickly
Not included: ongoing SEO work such as regular content writing, keyword research, advertising or promotion.

7  Website Delivery Time
 About 2 weeks
The first version of your website will be ready in approximately 2 weeks. This assumes that you
provide the required content, images and logo on time and share your feedback during development.
8  Changes After the First Version
 Up to 5 included
After the first version is published, up to 5 small changes are included at no extra cost, such as:
  Changing text
 Replacing an image
 Small layout adjustments
 Minor corrections
If you later want to add a new page, introduce a new feature or make a major change to the agreed
design, we will first discuss the requirement and any extra cost with you before we begin.
9  Support After Launch
 30 days free
For 30 days after your website is published, any issue or error related to the work delivered under
the agreed website scope will be fixed free of charge. New features, new pages or changes in
requirements during this period will be discussed separately.
10  Domain, Hosting & Outside Services
Domain (your website address): You have already purchased your domain, and we will connect it to
the new website. Renewal of the domain is paid by you directly to your domain provider.
Hosting (where your website is stored online): Hosting is the space on the internet where your
website is stored, so that people can visit it at any time. Hosting is included in the ₹15,000 for the
first year. After the first year it will be paid, and the amount will be decided based on your website's
traffic and resource usage at that time. We will tell you the amount before it is due.
Outside services: some features, such as showing customer reviews and the event locations map,
use services from other companies. Your ₹15,000 covers the normal amount of visitors and usage for a
business website. If at any time you need extra resources, or your website traffic grows beyond that,
we will inform you of the updated charges first, before making any change, and these charges
will be paid directly by you.
If you need a paid service from another company that we do not provide, you will pay that company
directly. We will inform you before using any such service.
Ownership: You own your domain name and all the content on your website.
11  Privacy & Legal Pages
Since your enquiry form collects customers' details, the website's privacy policy and related legal
pages will be prepared following the applicable government laws and norms.
