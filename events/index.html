The Timeline

The Lifecycle of an Eventina Commission

Our structured production cadence ensures that from initial mood boards to post-event breakdown, every transition is clear, transparent, and completely on schedule.

Phase 1 • Alignment

Brief & Spatial Audit

Venue walkthroughs, acoustic tests, regulatory feasibility, and budget framework finalization.

Phase 2 • Design

Scenography Blueprint

3D renders, floral palettes, stage drawings, lighting simulations, and vendor contracting.

Phase 3 • Rehearsal

Technical Dry Runs

Sound riders tested, cue sheets locked, artist hospitality prepped, and shadow concierges briefed.

Phase 4 • Showtime

Live Command Post

On-ground execution, live cue firing, discreet VIP care, and seamless post-event strike.
