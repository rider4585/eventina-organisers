## 1. The Physics of Heritage Acoustics {#physics}

Historic fortresses, havelis, and marble courtyards were designed centuries before amplified sound existed. Built from polished Makrana marble, solid quartzite, and lime plaster, their surfaces reflect up to 98% of incident sound waves. When an amateur event vendor brings standard PA loudspeakers into such spaces, high-frequency reflections bounce between parallel arches, converting wedding vows and emotional speeches into an echo chamber where nobody can understand a word.

At Eventina, acoustic engineering is treated with the same scientific rigour as structural architectural design. We conduct comprehensive computerized ray-tracing analyses of each venue to map out exact reflection paths and reverberation times ($RT_{60}$) before determining speaker placement.

---

## 2. Directional Column Arrays & Beamforming {#column-arrays}

Traditional box speakers spray audio in a broad, uncontrolled spherical pattern. By contrast, directional column line arrays utilize closely spaced micro-drivers to generate a razor-flat horizontal sound plane with almost zero vertical dispersion.

- **Precision Ear-Level Coverage:** Audio is directed strictly towards seated guests, bypassing high marble ceilings and carved jali screens.
- **Minimal Spill:** Guests sitting near the front experience clear, comfortable conversational levels, while guests 40 meters back hear with equal clarity and warmth.
- **Architectural Discretion:** Slim column enclosures finished in custom matte ivory or antique brass blend invisibly against palace stone columns.

---

## 3. Discreet Acoustic Absorption {#absorption}

Absorbing excess reverberation without disrupting the aesthetic dignity of a 400-year-old monument is an art form. We design bespoke acoustic absorption panels disguised as decorative velvet backdrops, hand-embroidered tapestry banners, and heavy raw silk portières. 

Strategically placed behind the main performance stage and along flanking colonnades, these unobtrusive treatments lower $RT_{60}$ down to a studio-grade 1.2 seconds, allowing every nuance of vocal performance and instrumental harmony to shine through with crystal clarity.
