## 1. The Death of the 200-Dish Buffet {#buffet-evolution}

The era of endless chafing dishes where gourmet curries turn lukewarm under sternos is rapidly coming to an end in high-end celebration design. Discerning guests no longer wish to stand in queues with plate in hand; they demand an intimate, seated culinary journey on par with the world’s three-Michelin-star restaurants.

At Eventina, gastronomy is conceived as theatrical narrative. We collaborate with celebrated master chefs from across the globe to curate 5 to 7-course plated degustations that reflect the host family's cultural roots while embracing cutting-edge contemporary presentation.

---

## 2. Narrative Courses & Royal Archives {#culinary-narrative}

Each dish served during an Eventina gala tells a specific story:

- **Lost Royal Recipes:** Recreating heritage recipes resurrected from royal palace kitchens in Rampur, Awadh, and Travancore, cooked using traditional slow-fire copper deghs.
- **Micro-Seasonal Sourcing:** Saffron from the valleys of Pampore, morels foraged in the Himalayas, and cold-pressed mustard oil crafted in limited small-batch pressings.
- **Sommelier Pairings:** Curating vintage champagne pairings for delicate canapés and grand cru Burgundies harmonized with royal slow-cooked venison or heritage wild mushroom galoutis.

---

## 3. Synchronized Service Protocol {#service-excellence}

A plated banquet for 400 guests requires impeccable service synchronization. Eventina trains banquet teams according to European royal butler standards:

- **Simultaneous Table Reveals:** Thirty servers enter the dining pavilion in choreographed unison, placing cloches on every seat simultaneously.
- **Silent Sommelier Pouring:** Glasses are refilled without disturbing ongoing speeches or subtle acoustic melodies.
- **Dietary Precision:** Custom allergen cards and bespoke personal menus printed in gold calligraphy on handmade linen paper for every guest with distinct preferences.
